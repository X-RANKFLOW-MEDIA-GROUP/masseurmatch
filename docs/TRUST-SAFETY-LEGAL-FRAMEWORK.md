# MasseurMatch Legal & Trust Safety Framework v1.0

Effective: August 10, 2026

## Platform position

MasseurMatch is a directory and technology platform. It does not provide massage or bodywork services, employ providers, arrange appointments, supervise user interactions, or guarantee providers. Providers are independent third parties responsible for their content, services, conduct, qualifications, pricing, scheduling, communications, transactions, and legal compliance.

## Absolute prohibition

MasseurMatch prohibits advertising, solicitation, promotion, facilitation, arrangement, or provision through the platform of prostitution, commercial sexual activity, trafficking, erotic services, or sexual services.

## Photo moderation standard

Professional shirtless, fitness, swimwear, wellness, workspace, and bodywork-related images may be allowed when non-explicit and not reasonably interpreted as advertising sexual services.

Reject or escalate content involving visible genitalia, deliberately emphasized genital areas, explicit nudity, sexual activity, simulated sexual activity, genital touching, sexual solicitation, coded commercial-sex language, exploitation indicators, or minors in sexualized contexts.

A visible torso alone is not prohibited. Review the complete context, including pose, captions, bio, advertised services, pricing language, reported communications, and account history.

## Verification rule

Verification means only that the specifically identified verification process was completed at a point in time. It is not endorsement, professional licensing, certification, background clearance, a guarantee of identity beyond the scope of the check, a safety guarantee, or a guarantee of future conduct.

## Provider responsibility

Unless MasseurMatch expressly states that a particular credential was verified, professional licenses, certifications, insurance, education, and regulatory standing are provider-supplied information. Users remain responsible for their own due diligence.

## User generated content

Providers remain responsible for content they submit and must possess the rights necessary to upload it. MasseurMatch moderates content for platform integrity and compliance but moderation approval does not transfer responsibility for the content to MasseurMatch or constitute endorsement.

## Operational rule

Legal policy and product enforcement must remain aligned. A written prohibition that is knowingly and systematically ignored creates avoidable risk. Moderation decisions should therefore be consistent, documented, reviewable, and tied to the policy version applied.

## Legal review

This framework is an operational baseline and should be reviewed by qualified U.S. counsel before being treated as a final legal opinion, especially regarding federal and state intermediary liability, FOSTA/SESTA, Section 230, 18 U.S.C. §§ 2257/2257A, privacy, mandatory reporting, evidence preservation, and state-specific massage/bodywork regulation.
